Egg Sandwich

:Kenji-López-Alt's egg salad sandwich. Photograph: Felicity Cloake for the Guardian

Description

The egg and mayonnaise sandwich with Aromat is a simple creamy sandwich made around crushed hard-boiled eggs, mixed with mayonnaise and a bit of seasoning. The egg mixture is spread between slices of soft bread, usually white or brown bread.

Ingredients

  • 2 Eggs
  • Mayonnaise
  • Aromat spice (or any desired seasoning spice)
  • Margarine/Butter
  • Water
  • 4 slices of bread

Steps

  1. Boil your 2 eggs in a small pot for 10 to 15 minutes. After that, put your pot aside and let it cool down with the water still inside.
  2. Spread butter on all 4 slices of bread for that savoury base flavour.
  3. Peel your eggs and put your boiled eggs in a small bowl. Crush your eggs with a fork until they are left to be crumbs.
  4. Sprinkle that seasoning of yours on the eggs, and add about 4 tablespoons of Mayonnaise and stir repeatedly. It is also recommended to add small extras such as chopped parsley, chives or black pepper.
  5. Finally, spread the egg mixture evenly on the slices of bread, assemble the sandwich by placing each 2 slices of bread together with the mixture in between and ENJOY!
